# IITM Software Engineering Course Alignment: May 2026

## Recommended Session Topic

**AI-Augmented Software Engineering for the IITM Project: From User Research to Tested APIs and Final Review**

Short version:

**Using AI Responsibly in the IITM Software Engineering Project**

## Course Project Context

Students must choose one of two project directions:

1. **Community Services Platform**
   - Apartment association
   - Sports club
   - Volunteer organization
   - Library
   - Cultural association
   - Student club

2. **Civic Services Platform**
   - Complaint management
   - Public facility booking
   - Waste collection requests
   - Community maintenance requests
   - Permit tracking

The sample repository in this folder uses the **Civic Services Platform: Complaint Management** direction.

## Common Course Expectations

Every team project must:

- Support at least two distinct user roles.
- Manage multiple interrelated workflows.
- Expose well-defined APIs for core functionality.
- Include automated tests for critical business logic.
- Be designed for future growth and change.
