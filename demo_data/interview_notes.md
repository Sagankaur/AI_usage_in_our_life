## Notes

A resident says they submit maintenance complaints in a WhatsApp group. They cannot tell whether anyone has accepted the request. Sometimes they post the same issue again because no status is visible.

The association manager says duplicate messages make it difficult to know how many unresolved issues remain.

A resident says one complaint was marked closed even though the leak returned the next day. They posted again in the group because they did not know whether the old complaint could be reopened.

The maintenance supervisor says repeated messages make it hard to see whether an issue is new, unresolved, or already assigned.
